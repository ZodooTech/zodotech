Industry Adaptations

Sector-Specific Odoo Applications.

Every industry has unique operational friction points. See how we architect Odoo modules, approval rules, and automated workflows to match specific vertical requirements.

Sector 01

Trading & Wholesale Distribution

Wholesale trading businesses operate on thin margins and fast inventory turns. Our Odoo customizations automate order allocation, calculate landed import costs accurately, and support tiered multi-currency pricing matrices.

Potential Applications:

  • • Automated landed cost distribution across sea/air freight shipments
  • • Multi-currency sales quotations with live forex rate updates
  • • Customer-specific volume rebate calculations and credit limit enforcement
  • • Direct vendor dropship routing with automated purchase generation
Target Modules·Sales · Inventory · Purchase · Accounting

Operational Benefit:

Trading firms gain immediate visibility into true gross profit margins per product line after factoring in freight, import tariffs, and storage expenses.

Sector 02

Logistics, Freight & Transportation

Standard ERP systems lack native freight models. We build custom consignment architectures that track bills of lading, container status updates, driver assignments, and port clearance milestones.

Potential Applications:

  • • Master Waybill & House Bill of Lading generation directly from Odoo
  • • Container tracking status board with milestone date alerts
  • • Automated 3PL carrier tariff comparisons and invoice reconciliation
  • • REST API connectivity for driver mobile apps and GPS tracking feeds
Target Modules·Custom Freight Suite · Fleet · Invoicing

Operational Benefit:

Centralizes freight operations and billing into one screen, eliminating reliance on separate tracking sheets and manual dispatch spreadsheets.

Sector 03

Professional Services & Consultancies

Consultancies, engineering practices, and digital agencies require strict alignment between recorded employee hours and customer billing milestones.

Potential Applications:

  • • Timesheet validation rules with automatic manager approval triggers
  • • Fixed-price milestone invoicing tied to project deliverable sign-offs
  • • Consultant billable rate cards and margin tracking per project task
  • • Real-time team utilization dashboards and project burn-rate forecasts
Target Modules·Project · Timesheets · Subscriptions · Invoicing

Operational Benefit:

Accurately bills every billable hour, eliminates invoicing disputes, and provides project managers with immediate project profit metrics.

Sector 04

Manufacturing & Discrete Assembly

Manufacturing operations demand rigorous Bill of Materials (BOM) management, work center capacity scheduling, and component traceability.

Potential Applications:

  • • Multi-level Bill of Materials with scrap rate calculations and routing steps
  • • Work center tablet interface for shop-floor operators to log completion times
  • • Serial and lot number tracking for end-to-end component genealogy
  • • Subcontracting order dispatch and raw material replenishment triggers
Target Modules·MRP · Quality · Maintenance · Barcode

Operational Benefit:

Prevents production stoppages due to unrecorded raw material shortages and provides auditable component traceability for compliance audits.

Sector 05

Retail & Omnichannel E-Commerce

Retailers operating both physical storefronts and digital webshops require real-time stock synchronization to prevent overselling and streamline fulfillment.

Potential Applications:

  • • Unified inventory synchronization between Odoo POS, Shopify, and Amazon
  • • Click-and-collect fulfillment workflows across retail branch networks
  • • Automated stock reserve locks during active online checkout sessions
  • • Unified customer loyalty points and gift voucher ledger across all channels
Target Modules·Point of Sale · Inventory · E-Commerce · Loyalty

Operational Benefit:

Eliminates the risk of selling out-of-stock items, unifies customer histories, and centralizes financial reporting across online and in-store channels.

Sector 06 & 07

Real Estate & Healthcare Operations

Specialized sectors require bespoke data models for unit leases, preventive facility maintenance, medical sample chains-of-custody, and audit compliance.

Potential Applications:

  • • Property lease contract schedules with automated rent escalation calculation
  • • Tenant maintenance request portal with contractor dispatch workflows
  • • Reagent expiry tracking and laboratory sample chain-of-custody logs
  • • Medical equipment preventive maintenance scheduling with certification records
Target Modules·Custom Lease Models · Maintenance · Field Service

Operational Benefit:

Provides compliance-ready record keeping, prevents missed lease escalation dates, and automates vendor work orders.
