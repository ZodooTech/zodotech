Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 2026. This editable policy explains how ZodoTech handles project inquiry submissions and website data.

Owner Notice: This privacy policy template is provided for operational transparency. Please review and verify it against your specific jurisdictional requirements and actual business practices before final deployment.

1. Information We Collect

When you submit a project inquiry via our website form, we collect the information you voluntarily provide: your full name, business email address, company name, Odoo version, project subject, and project description. We do not collect passwords, payment card numbers, or unnecessary personal data.

2. How We Use Submitted Information

Information collected through our contact form is used strictly to:

  • • Evaluate your technical project requirements and determine feasibility.
  • • Respond to your consultation inquiry via email.
  • • Prepare a formal proposal, scope outline, or quotation.

We never sell, rent, or trade your contact details or project descriptions to third-party marketing companies.

3. Contact Form Processing

Our website contact form utilizes FormSubmit (formsubmit.co) as an email forwarding service to deliver form submissions securely to our engineering team mailbox (odoodeveloper10@gmail.com). FormSubmit acts as a data processor for the sole purpose of message transmission.

4. Data Retention & Security

We retain business communications only for as long as necessary to facilitate ongoing business consultations or fulfill contractual deliverables. We apply standard security practices to protect communication channels against unauthorized access.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to request access to the personal data we hold about you, request corrections, or request that your contact details and inquiry records be deleted from our mailbox. To exercise these rights, please email us directly at odoodeveloper10@gmail.com.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact our team via email at:
